How does a simple mark become information?

DISCRETE explores five systems of communication and information: Braille, Morse code, dot matrix printing, punched cards, and QR codes.

The word discrete refers to separate, individual elements that come together to form a larger system. Although each of these five systems works differently, they all share the same principle: simple units can be arranged to create meaning, whether through touch, sound, print, or machine-readable patterns.
