1960s–1970s / Impact Printing / Ink & Paper

Constructing Letters from Dots. A printing technology that constructs letters and images from individual ink dots, transforming digital instructions into physical marks.

02Category02.1Print

Dot matrix printing emerged as computers required flexible ways to print text and graphics. Pin-based impact printers became widely used during the 1970s and 1980s, offering an alternative to printers with fixed character shapes.

From Dots to Characters

A printhead uses small metal pins to strike an ink ribbon against paper. As the head moves, individual dots form letters and images within a grid, commonly using 5 × 7 or 9 × 9 character matrices.
